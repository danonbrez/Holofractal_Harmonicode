# 11. Ordered and noncommutative composition

Rotation and fold history MUST preserve operation order.

```text
R(i,j,a) * R(k,l,b)
```

is not assumed equal to:


Commutation MAY be recorded only when proven by the applicable plane relation and state constraints.

The conformance suite MUST include both:

- a noncommuting overlapping-plane example;
