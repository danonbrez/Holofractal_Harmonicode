# 6. Permanent instruction fabric

The permanent instruction address SHALL be:

\[

\]

where:

\[
c\in[0,80],\qquad
o\in[0,63],\qquad
s\in[0,5183].
\]

The inverse SHALL be:

\[
c=\left\lfloor\frac{s}{64}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
o=s\bmod64.
\]

```text

cell 1:  operation 0 ... operation 63
```

Instruction identity SHALL remain immutable across state versions, cache rebuilds, workers, architectures, restarts, and replays.

The canonical sequential instruction pointer SHALL be:

\[

# 7. G243 control fabric

\[

\qquad |G_{243}|=243.
\]

The instruction/control projection address SHALL be:

\[

\]

\[
s\in[0,5183],\qquad
