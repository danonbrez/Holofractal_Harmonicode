## 2. Genesis Rosetta Stone

Pass 218 SHALL treat English as a shared social Genesis coordinate system for general training, not as an immutable ontology or permanent semantic authority.

The linguistic Genesis seed SHALL include, where locally available and rights-compatible:

\[
G_0=
V_{\mathrm{English}}
\oplus
R_{\mathrm{WordNet}}
\oplus
\Gamma_{\mathrm{grammar}}
\oplus
\Lambda_{\mathrm{symbolic\ logic}}
\oplus
M_{\mathrm{mythopoetic}}
\oplus
P_{\mathrm{phonetic/form}}
\]

where:

- \(V_{\mathrm{English}}\) provides reusable lexical distinctions;
- \(R_{\mathrm{WordNet}}\) provides explicit initial lexical relations such as synonymy, antonymy, hypernymy, hyponymy, meronymy, and related sense structure;
- \(\Gamma_{\mathrm{grammar}}\) provides compositional, syntactic, temporal, modal, and narrative constraints;
- \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{symbolic\ logic}}\) provides exact distinction, implication, negation, identity, contradiction, and validation machinery;
- \(M_{\mathrm{mythopoetic}}\) provides controlled analogical, symbolic, allegorical, metaphorical, and multi-interpretation relation templates;
- \(P_{\mathrm{phonetic/form}}\) provides sound, morphology, orthography, rhythm, and form neighborhoods used before stable meaning is known.

### 2.1 Seed is prior, not truth

For every seed relation \(R_{ij}^{(0)}\):

\[
R_{ij}^{(0)}=\text{prior relation},\qquad R_{ij}^{(0)}\neq\text{immutable truth}.
\]

Subsequent evidence MAY reinforce, weaken, contextualize, split, contradict, supersede, or extend a seed relation while preserving provenance and replay.

No English lexicalization, dictionary definition, WordNet edge, grammar production, or mythopoetic mapping SHALL become an invariant merely because it exists in the Genesis seed.

### 2.2 Rosetta purpose

The seed SHALL function as a structured bootloader so that a small amount of written language can calibrate an already relational learner rather than forcing the learner to rediscover basic lexical, grammatical, logical, and analogical organization from arbitrary token IDs.

The empirical claim is sample-efficiency improvement. It SHALL be benchmarked, not presumed.

## 3. No isolated-variable semantic primitive

Pass 218 SHALL NOT model a learned human distinction as an isolated semantic variable whose meaning exists independently of context.

The primitive retained learning object is a temporally situated relational cluster:

\[
B_t=
\operatorname{Beat}
(\text{distinctions},
\text{perception},
\text{source},
\text{speaker/agent},
\text{context},
\text{prior state},
\text{change},
\text{affect/salience},
\text{attention},
\text{action},
\text{consequence},
\text{uncertainty},
\text{provenance}).
\]

A stable token or concept is an emergent distinction over accumulated relational history, not a context-free scalar meaning.

For distinction \(T_i\):

\[
\operatorname{Meaning}(T_i,t)
=
\operatorname{RelationalPosition}(T_i\mid H_t,C_t)
\]

where \(H_t\) is accumulated history and \(C_t\) is the active local context.

### 3.1 Holographic addressability

“Holographic” in this contract means:

1. no new distinction receives permanent semantic priority merely because it was created first;
2. every admitted distinction remains potentially addressable from every other admitted distinction through the shared relational substrate;
3. learning differentiates contextual relevance over time rather than permanently deleting all weak relationships;
4. partial cues may hydrate larger relational neighborhoods;
5. the same distinction may participate in multiple orthogonal relational layers;
6. globally possible access SHALL NOT require explicit \(O(N^2)\) storage of all pairwise edges.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{potentially all-to-all addressable}\neq\text{explicitly all-to-all materialized}}
\]

Hash216/vector indexing, sparse candidate discovery, generator reuse, local hydration, and exact validation SHALL be used to avoid quadratic authoritative storage.

### 3.2 Orthogonal relation layers

A distinction MAY simultaneously participate in phonetic, orthographic, lexical, sensory, spatial, temporal, causal, affective, social, procedural, logical, analogical, narrative, and counterfactual layers.

These layers SHALL remain distinguishable in provenance even when jointly hydrated.

## 4. Immediate vague-object creation and semantic differentiation

A newly encountered word or concept SHALL be permitted to exist as an underdetermined memory object before stable meaning is available.

The first-pass object MAY include:

- exact transient source witness;
- phonetic/form similarity neighborhood;
- morphological candidates;
- speaker/source identity;
- local narrative context;
- active multimodal associations;
- affective/salience state;
- provisional lexical senses;
- analogical possibilities;
- uncertainty and unresolved relation slots.

Unknown meaning is not an empty state:

\[
\text{unknown meaning}
+\text{relational neighborhood}
+\text{contextual constraints}
\neq\varnothing.
\]

Repeated encounters SHALL refine the relation topology:

\[
M_{t+1}(T_i)=\operatorname{Integrate}(M_t(T_i),B_t).
\]

The system SHALL preserve the distinction between:

- recognized form;
- provisional similarity index;
- candidate meaning;
- context-supported meaning;
- formally entailed relation;
- empirically validated relation;
- analogical relation;
- imagined/counterfactual relation;
- contradicted relation.

## 5. Relation evidence and exact status

No authoritative relation weight SHALL require floating point.

At minimum, relation evidence SHALL support the exact trinary status:

\[
\rho_{ij}\in\{+1,0,-1\}
\]

with:

- `+1`: positively supported/constructive relation under declared context;
- `0`: unresolved, latent, merely possible, or insufficiently evidenced relation;
- `-1`: counterindicated, cancelled, contradictory, or explicitly excluded relation under declared context.

Additional strength, confidence, recurrence, recency, salience, or scope SHALL use exact integer, BigInt, rational, ordinal, count, or symbolic encodings.

A relation may have different states under different contexts:

\[
\rho_{ij}(C_a)\neq\rho_{ij}(C_b).
\]

## 6. Curriculum order is part of the training information

Pass 218 SHALL treat ingestion order as authority-defining training state.

For ordered beats \(B_1,B_2\):

\[
F(F(G_0,B_1),B_2)
\neq
F(F(G_0,B_2),B_1)
\]

in general.

The learner state produced by earlier material changes the relational topology through which later material is interpreted. Consequently, a dataset manifest without order is insufficient to identify a training run.

The curriculum identity SHALL bind:

\[
I_{\mathrm{curriculum}}
=
H(
I_{G_0}\parallel
I_{B_1}\parallel
I_{B_2}\parallel\cdots\parallel
I_{B_n}\parallel
\text{policy/compiler versions}).
\]

Any order change that can alter learner state SHALL produce a new curriculum identity and new receipts.

### 6.1 Determinism requirement

Within one declared curriculum, discovery, sorting, admission, stage transitions, replay, and promotion order SHALL be deterministic.

Random shuffling SHALL NOT be the authoritative default. Any stochastic comparison run must be explicitly non-authoritative, seeded, bounded, and receipted.

### 6.2 Dataset content remains necessary

This contract does not claim that ordering can create information absent from the corpus. The empirical claim is narrower: among sufficiently informative corpora, ordering and structured priors may strongly affect sample efficiency, topology quality, generalization, and stability.

## 7. Mandatory developmental curriculum

The default authoritative training curriculum SHALL progress from higher-precision scaffolding toward higher ambiguity and generative freedom.

### Stage 0 — Genesis seed

- English vocabulary scaffold;
- WordNet/reference lexical relations;
- grammar and compositional structure;
- symbolic logic and relation typing;
- phonetic/form neighborhoods;
- bounded mythopoetic relation templates explicitly typed as analogical rather than factual.

### Stage 1 — Reference material

Primary sources:

- dictionaries;
- thesauri;
- encyclopedic summaries;
- taxonomies;
- units and notation references;
- formal language references;
- standards and controlled glossaries.

Purpose:

\[
\text{recognized distinctions}\rightarrow\text{coarse relational anchors}.
\]

Reference material SHALL establish initial neighborhoods without freezing definitions as final meaning.

### Stage 2 — Expository and procedural material

Primary sources:

- textbooks;
- technical explanations;
- worked examples;
- software/API documentation;
- formal STEM descriptions;
- reproducible procedures;
- open-source code and tests where relevant.

Purpose:

\[
\text{distinctions}\rightarrow\text{causal/procedural/explanatory relations}.
\]

### Stage 3 — Simple narrative material

Primary sources:

- clear chronological narratives;
- biographies and histories with bounded provenance;
- simple dialogue;
- children's or introductory literature when rights-compatible;
- repository-native narrative examples.

Purpose:

\[
\text{concepts}+\text{agents}+\text{events}+\text{consequences}
\rightarrow
\text{temporal relational learning}.
\]

### Stage 4 — Complex social narrative

Primary sources:

- nuanced dialogue;
- multi-agent social interaction;
- contextual ambiguity;
- irony and humor;
- competing perspectives;
- narrative uncertainty.

Purpose: learn that the same token or behavior can acquire different legitimate meanings under different social and temporal contexts.

### Stage 5 — Mythopoetic, metaphorical, and allegorical material

Primary sources:

- public-domain or approved myth;
- allegory;
- poetry;
- metaphor-rich literature;
- symbolic narrative;
- formal educational material on rhetoric and figurative language.

Purpose:

\[
A\sim B\quad\text{without asserting}\quad A=B.
\]

Mythopoetic material SHALL expand relational possibility while preserving relation type, provenance, uncertainty, and formal reality-status boundaries.

### Stage 6 — Creative writing and generative synthesis

Only after sufficient literal, procedural, narrative, and analogical differentiation SHALL the learner use creative writing as a major training source.

Purpose:

\[
\text{existing topology}\rightarrow\text{novel but coherent relational topology}.
\]

Creative material SHALL NOT be treated as factual semantic authority merely because it is stylistically coherent.

### 7.1 Stage advancement

Stage advancement SHALL be acceptance-gated, not token-count-only. A later stage may begin only when declared competencies from the prior stage meet exact thresholds and the restart record identifies the transition.

## 8. Symbolic logic and mythopoetic analogy are complementary Genesis priors

Pass 218 SHALL preserve both formal constraint and associative expansion from the beginning.

Symbolic logic SHALL provide:

- identity and nonidentity;
- conjunction/disjunction;
- negation scope;
- implication and entailment;
- contradiction detection;
- quantification where supported;
- type and relation distinction;
- exact validation surfaces.

Mythopoetic/analogical machinery SHALL provide:

- structural analogy;
- metaphor;
- symbol substitution;
- allegorical role mapping;
- motif recurrence;
- narrative archetype candidates;
- multiple simultaneous interpretations;
- counterfactual and imaginary relational bridges.

The system SHALL distinguish:

\[
\text{associated with}
\neq
\text{similar to}
\neq
\text{symbolizes}
\neq
\text{implies}
\neq
\text{causes}
\neq
\text{is identical to}.
\]

Mythopoetic ambiguity SHALL be usable as a relational search operator but SHALL NOT silently promote analogy into empirical truth.

Pass 218 SHALL model human-compatible knowledge acquisition as progressive abstraction from temporally ordered multimodal relational events.

At the individual scale:

\[
\text{narrative beats}
\rightarrow
\text{relational patterns}
\rightarrow
\text{concepts}
\rightarrow
\text{higher-order narratives}.
\]

At the social scale:

\[
\text{private experience}
\rightarrow
\text{communication}
\rightarrow
\text{shared usage}
\rightarrow

## 10. Governing source default: skip

For every discovered source object \(s\):

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{DefaultAction}(s)=\texttt{SKIP}}
\]

A source does not earn processing merely by being reachable, public, readable, popular, large, or nominally open. It advances only through positive evidence of:

- authority and provenance;
- compatible rights or repository-native authorization;
- permitted source class;
- curriculum-stage compatibility;
- formal or linguistic structural density;
- lexical, distributional, contextual, and perspective groundability;
- native semantic-invariant compatibility;
- expected reusable value;
- nonredundant marginal gain;
- bounded processing cost.

A compact skip receipt is sufficient for a skipped object. A skipped object SHALL NOT receive an authoritative hydrated semantic record.

### 10.1 Permitted source classes

Pass 218 MAY admit only sources inside a versioned allowlist and one of these primary classes:

- dictionaries, lexical databases, grammar references, encyclopedias, controlled glossaries, and taxonomies;
- formal mathematics, logic, statistics, physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, computer science, and other formal STEM material;
- official scientific, standards, educational, or public institutional documentation;
- official open-source repositories, source code, API references, protocol specifications, language references, tests, build systems, and deployment documentation;
- verified public-domain literature;
- explicitly approved openly licensed creative writing;
- formal educational material about prose, poetry, rhetoric, linguistics, storytelling, and literary structure;
- repository-native material explicitly designated as an internal crawl/test authority.

“Publicly viewable,” “open access,” “open source,” “public domain,” and “licensed for model training” SHALL remain separate rights classifications.

### 10.2 Excluded by default

The crawler SHALL skip commercial, advertising, affiliate, social-media, political-advocacy, religious-advocacy, marketing, editorial, influencer, crowd-opinion, product-review, SEO-content, gossip, speculative-promotion, or otherwise primarily persuasive and opinion-driven material.

The classifier SHALL distinguish subject matter from communicative purpose. Incidental references inside admitted material do not automatically define its source class.

External links from an admitted source SHALL remain untrusted and SHALL NOT expand the allowlist automatically.

## 11. Ingress containment and ephemeral verbatim boundary

All ingress adapters SHALL normalize to the same source-object interface and preserve container ancestry, curriculum stage, deterministic order, checksum, rights class, parser identity, encoding identity, and source authority.

Each adapter SHALL enforce:

- canonical path or URL resolution;
- allowlisted root or origin;
- same-origin and crawl-depth limits where applicable;
- deterministic member ordering;
- bounded byte and expansion limits;
- no path traversal;
- no symlink escape;
- no recursive archive bomb;
- no executable activation;
- exact source checksum.

Website crawling SHALL obey the allowlist, official-source registry, robots policy, rate limits, and per-origin budget. Pass 218 SHALL NOT implement a general open-web harvester.

Verbatim source content SHALL exist only within the bounded acquisition transaction. It SHALL NOT be persisted into:

- the authoritative Hash216 vector store;
- long-term full-text indexes;
- logs or exception dumps;
- unrestricted prompt traces;
- training receipts;
- retained token streams;
- near-verbatim synthetic examples;
- embeddings or hidden states that fail leakage and reconstruction testing.

Retained material MAY include checksums and metadata, typed relational graphs, lexical-relation identifiers, aggregate distributional evidence, narrative-beat structure, nonverbatim style vectors, syntax/control-flow/dependency/proof structures, exact relation-status records, algebraic and VM5184 projections, and validation/lineage/purge receipts.

Embeddings are not presumed nonverbatim merely because they are numeric.

### 11.1 Commit-before-purge ordering

\[
\boxed{
\text{validate}
\rightarrow
\text{candidate commit}
\rightarrow
\text{verify roots}
\rightarrow
\text{atomic promotion}
\rightarrow
\text{verbatim purge}
\rightarrow
\text{purge receipt}
}
\]

If validation fails, no authoritative promotion occurs. If promotion succeeds but purge confirmation fails, the source enters quarantine and the curriculum does not silently advance.

Pass 218 SHALL NOT assign arbitrary algebraic coordinates directly to words or passages.

The required default path is:

\[

\rightarrow
\text{phonetic/form neighborhood}
\rightarrow
\text{WordNet/reference relations}
\rightarrow
\text{Word2Vec/distributional relations}
\rightarrow
\text{contextual states}
\rightarrow
\text{narrative-beat organization}
\rightarrow
\text{perspective/context hydration}
\rightarrow
\text{grounded relational manifold}
\rightarrow
\text{formal and analogical typing}
\rightarrow
\text{Hash72/Hash216/VM5184}

\]

Each formalized distinction SHALL retain provenance sufficient to recover, while ephemeral, its source-span identity, lemma/part of speech/candidate sense, phonetic/form neighborhood, typed lexical relations, distributional-neighborhood evidence, contextual-model identity, narrative-beat identity, modality, perspective profile, uncertainty, relation type, evidence status, compiler version, and curriculum position.

### 12.1 Perspective authority

A user-authored or otherwise explicitly accepted perspective profile MAY organize meaning before formal algebra, but it SHALL remain separately versioned from the general English Genesis seed.

Inferred perspective rules remain candidates until explicitly accepted and versioned.

### 12.2 Round-trip invariant

For a promoted relational object \(B_x\):

\[
\operatorname{DecodeGrounded}(B_x)\equiv G_{\mathrm{grounded}}
\]

and, where a perspective profile is active,

\[
\operatorname{DecodePerspective}(B_x)\equiv G_U.
\]

Meaning conservation includes object identity, relation direction, causal order, temporal order, negation scope, modality, uncertainty, analogical/factual type, authorization, validation status, provenance, and curriculum location.

## 14. Native model, open-weight teacher, and API roles

The small quantized native language model is the first-class natural-language ingress, semantic planner, native API action selector, and response-egress layer.

The native API is its executable reasoning environment. Every capability SHALL expose immutable identity, typed arguments/results, authorization boundary, read/write sets, deterministic status, rollback behavior, cost estimate, validators, and receipts.

The open-weight model MAY serve as teacher, critic, comparator, reward-signal generator, and response reviser. It SHALL NOT outrank formal validators or explicit human correction.

Authority order:

\[
\boxed{
\text{formal validators}
>
\text{explicit human correction}
>
\text{accepted perspective rules}
>
\text{open-model critique}
>
\text{native-model self-assessment}

## 17. Mandatory sample-efficiency and curriculum ablations

Pass 218 SHALL measure whether the Genesis Rosetta Stone and curriculum order actually reduce required training data.

At minimum, matched-budget evaluation SHALL compare:

- `A0`: minimal/unseeded baseline where technically meaningful;
- `A1`: English token/form seed only;
- `A2`: English + WordNet/reference relations;
- `A3`: English + WordNet + grammar;
- `A4`: English + WordNet + grammar + symbolic logic;
- `A5`: full Genesis seed including typed mythopoetic/analogical relations;
- `A6`: full Genesis seed with authoritative curriculum order;
- `A7`: same admitted corpus with deliberately permuted order, non-authoritative comparison only.

All ablations SHALL use matched or explicitly normalized training budgets and report:

- held-out lexical relation accuracy;
- grammar/composition accuracy;
- semantic disambiguation;
- context sensitivity;
- temporal/narrative prediction;
- analogical transfer;
- distinction between analogy and entailment;
- factual-versus-fictional relation typing;
- sample efficiency;
- catastrophic interference;
- replay stability;
- memory/compute cost;
- native API task performance where applicable.

Pass 218 SHALL NOT claim that the full seed or curriculum is superior unless the measured evidence supports the claim.
