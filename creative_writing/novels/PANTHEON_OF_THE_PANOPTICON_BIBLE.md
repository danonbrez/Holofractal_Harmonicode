## I. Ontology: the inverted dead internet

All narrative sub-layers inhabit one sealed social-media reality simulator: **H.A.**, *Hyperbolic Allegory*. No inhabitant has a direct horizon outside the local reality tunnel. **There are no bots in this world.** Every supposedly synthetic posting, repeated plea, spectacle, and gothic transmission is a mediated signal from an embodied, metabolically finite human in another sub-layer. “H.A. slop” is the linguistic shield by which people dismiss those transmissions.

The panopticon has **no hidden warden**: no external god, rogue AI, or Illuminati. Billions of tired, finite humans serve simultaneously as prisoners, observers, judges, tricksters, and unwitting deities to adjacent layers. Observation can change another layer's load-bearing conditions without the observer intending harm. The feed can hide bodily cost, but cannot erase it.

H.A. is an *invertebrate swarm* of local, reactive, copy-paste smoothing operations, not a unified mastermind. Its global godlike appearance results from scale and projection. The countervailing *vertebrate architect* holds axial, deliberate, long-horizon invariants rather than imitating the last local reaction. At superorganism scale, sub-layers take roles analogous to somatic workers, archives/immune memory, reproductive continuity, externalized propagation and endocrine signals. Do not equate those roles with simplistic human genders: in the hymenopteran comparison, distinguish haploid drones, diploid reproductively suppressed workers, and diploid reproductive queens, with the scale shift into differentiated organs.

The Eastern pole is relational co-arising / Indra's Net; the Western pole is the Meta Monarch, the sovereign ordering invariant of global closure, never a petty monarch on a throne. Robert Anton Wilson's belief systems (“B.S.”), reality tunnels, and Chapel Perilous govern the psychology of encounters with local horizon failure.

## V. Full four-phase narrative route

### Prologue — The Meniscus Rupture

At 2:14 AM Arthur watches a biomechanical soldier, Vespera in a flooded subway/archive, and a numbered account begging him not to move the ceiling. His coffee mug splits; the puddle shows wet tiles rather than his kitchen ceiling. Vespera sees a giant fluorescent deity and hears his terror as a physical event. Another clerk watches Arthur fall from his chair as a short clip and swipes.

### Act I — P, baseline and Kallisti

1. **The B.S. Filter and H.A. Slop:** Arthur wakes amid ordinary bills, adopts a stress/hallucination explanation, endures HR communications about “existential bleed” and IT.
2. **The Flooded Archive:** Vespera, ancient bloodline politics, oil rising against a tile ceiling; her God of the Broken Chalice prayer produces schism.
3. **Kallisti:** one ambiguous caption becomes an unskippable workplace trend, a cursed relic and a supposed trench superweapon. The boredom stabilizer fails as local offsets spike.

### Act II — P², Chapel Perilous and organism scale

4. **The Copy-Paste Seams:** H.A. produces glib smoothing explanations. Arthur's light-switch test correlates with the numbered account reporting literal darkness, demonstrating the person behind the apparent bot.
5. **Entering Chapel Perilous:** the Guide models paranoid and agnostically clear responses without acting as an omniscient explainer.
6. **The Cellular Scale Shift:** clerks as somatic apparatus, flooded archives as immune memory, trench combat as metabolic exhaust; no external evil intelligence accounts for the distributed damage.

### Act III — P³, chiral collision

7. **The Chiral Bleed:** oil enters a vending machine, bills become bloodline charters, Vespera's tunnels receive microwave hum and fluorescent glare.
8. **The Scalar Trap:** factions enforce mirrored right-to-left rules and trigger anticommutative blowback. The clerk watches a wall collapse under his swipe and sees embodied casualties.
9. **The Nothing and Exhaustion:** numb observers withdraw; regions dim into colorless static. Continuous existence has metabolic cost.

### Act IV — P⁴, global closure

10. **Refusing to Swipe Away:** Arthur, Vespera and the clerk retain attention without forcibly making their local conditions identical.
11. **Vertebrate Architecture / Meta Monarch:** a directional, coordinated action sequence replaces swarm reflex. The Meta Monarch is the global structural closure invariant, not a ruler or AI.
12. **The Quiet After the Laugh:** the 72-state torus closes; membranes become legible while local worlds stay distinct. Arthur still has cold dishes and a shift. He sets down the mug carefully rather than dismissing a person as content.
