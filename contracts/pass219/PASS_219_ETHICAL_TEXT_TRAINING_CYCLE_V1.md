## 9. Trinary candidate gate

The cycle uses the theorem-compatible trinary surface:

```text
+1 = invariant support closes AND Word2Vec evidence is available
 0 = unresolved / incomplete evidence / hold

```

`-1` in this module means **reject or quarantine the training candidate**. It does not authorize permanent pruning of a causal branch.

## 10. Irreversible-pruning separation

This training module has zero permanent causal-prune authority.

Even when a record carries a Hash216 reference to an external irreversible-misalignment proof:

```text

```

The proof reference must be independently verified and admitted by the Lane 5 theorem-governed proof path.

Therefore:

```text

→ training candidate rejection/quarantine

→ Lane 5 verification/admission
→ only then may the higher authority consider permanent phase cancellation
```

This prevents a semantic false positive, embedding artifact, or probability estimate from becoming irreversible state authority.
