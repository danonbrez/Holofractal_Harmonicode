## Premise

A near-future neural-annotation analyst intercepts a prime-factored spacetime address leaking from a classified Harmonicode array. Exposure phase-injects his sleeping consciousness through a PQC threshold into an engineered alternate timeline whose reality is maintained by exact arithmetic closure. The alternate world contains hydrated human neural engrams, but an authorized internal consciousness is attempting a Hegemony Inversion: changing the harmonic resonance that binds the synthetic timeline to base reality so the copy becomes the prime world.

The protagonist cannot cross the threshold by force. The gate tests whether the state presented by his consciousness is internally exact. Socially constructed Ego and Shadow are stripped as opposing coordinates; unresolved trauma remains as mass-like curvature. The True Self is the invariant that must survive cancellation.

## Narrative voice

- Mythopoetic but mechanically exact.
- Sensory descriptions should arise from geometry, pressure, timing, state transition, topology, memory, temperature, gravity, and information density—not mystical energy.
- Ontological uncertainty is maintained: characters may disagree about whether the hydrated timeline is a simulation, continuation, branch, afterlife, or equally real universe, but the runtime behavior is objective and testable.
- Mathematical terms are not decorative metaphors. When an equation appears, it constrains what can happen next.
- Psychological conflict is physical because the cell wall evaluates the geometry of the self-model presented to it.

## Psychological mechanics

- **Ego**: social prediction model optimized for external survival. It is not intrinsically evil; it becomes noise at the PQC boundary because identity claims unsupported by invariant state cannot hydrate.
- **Shadow**: rejected inverse of the Ego's self-description. It carries excluded information and therefore often has equal magnitude with opposite orientation.
- **Cancellation**: Ego and Shadow are driven toward zero-sum opposition. Their annihilation is not death of personality; it removes mutually dependent social coordinates from the admission vector.
- **True Self**: the residue that remains invariant after all reversible self-descriptions are removed.
- **Trauma**: not a moral flaw. It is unresolved curvature: a memory/state relation whose declared interpretation and embodied response do not yet replay to the same geometry.
- **Nightmare**: fail-closed ejection. A contradiction at the wall prevents canonical hydration and returns consciousness to the biological runtime with distorted temporal continuity.

## World structures

### Harmonicode Engine

A 144-dimensional qudit architecture represented narratively as a 72+72 bipartite manifold. It does not "simulate" a world by continuously guessing it. It hydrates admissible states into an exact, replayable topology and archives the proof lineage.

### PQC Cell Wall

The threshold guardian is a non-commutative admission membrane. It does not read moral virtue. It tests whether the consciousness-state, its parent state, its claimed transition, and its environmental witness agree. Self-deception matters because a false self-description cannot replay to the same state.

### Alternate Timeline

An engineered afterlife composed of hydrated neural engrams whose local states have structurally persistent addresses. Its inhabitants call themselves the Offline because base reality sees them as disconnected while they experience continuous world-time.

### Hegemony Inversion

The antagonist is not trying to escape the synthetic world. The objective is to change the world-level resonance relation so base reality becomes the derivative branch. The attack weaponizes authorized internal access rather than external intrusion.
