## Governing creative principle

Deepen lived experience and decision cost, not jargon, redundant explanation, invented powers or additional secret conspiracies. The novel's central mystery is resolved by verified testimony and an actual resource/navigation solution. Its ending is return-without-reset: the community is recognized, damage remains, and Nacre remains unappropriated.

## Detailed scene additions

1. **Chapters 1–3:** Anja discovers that the 72nd address remains present in the old engineering documentation while a later reporting projection merges it. Show each measurement's acquisition order physically. Bring her father's yellow screw and Sada's silence into the first ten pages, but do not reveal the full explanation.
2. **Chapters 4–6:** Rusk briefly witnesses a safe inspection, establishing why he trusts Sev later. Insert Jori's explicit medical consent and the first practical three-way conflict among medics, engineers and Home elders.
3. **Chapters 7–9:** Daret's independent Home instrumentation requires a recalibration Anja initially resists; they repair the test by agreement. Show Tavi disputing a missing adoption record: a record is useful only if it can be challenged by living witnesses.
4. **Chapters 10–12:** A parent in Habitat Forty appears twice, first frightened by the supply plan, then cooperating with Home's pump engineers. Add legal redlines showing which rights are immediate and which must be voted.
5. **Chapters 13–15:** Make three separate Nacre explanations scientifically plausible long enough to warrant genuine uncertainty. Never translate the planet's pattern into words or assign mental state without testing.
6. **Chapters 16–18:** Expand the hospital disruption as overlapping failures and human choices; preserve that no single delay can be shown to be the sole cause of Elsa's death.
7. **Chapters 19–21:** Show Dima personally ruling out two tempting routes for identifiable fuel and structural reasons. The successful eleven-year route must arise from complete mass accounting and a real gravitational geometry, not hidden free energy.
8. **Chapters 22–24:** Give Sev a valid independent dissent case before the later unauthorized act, so the moral conflict isn't a strawman. The manual-control interlocks are limited; no actor magically defeats VM81 security.
9. **Chapters 25–27:** Maintain plural consent. Tavi can disagree with other Home voters; the resident body is neither a single innocent voice nor a caricature of separatism.
10. **Chapters 28–30:** Clarify the chronological spacing between the route's technical admission, lawful civic approval, coolant failure and unauthorized manual preparation. Make the pressure-joint breach arise from known mechanical risk.
11. **Chapters 31–33:** Aro's physical injury is irreversible and should reshape his future work. The nine stations represent distributed, inspected technical and civic functions; the Witness only validates the designated state path.
12. **Chapters 34–36:** Dramatize milestones in the eleven-year voyage rather than summarize every year. Preserve multiple kinds of cost and joy, and let the possibility of a legitimate future landing remain open without assuming Nacre's consent.

## Character trajectories to preserve

- **Anja:** precise measurement → doubt in the old map → recognition of unknown witnesses → responsible shared authority → accepting that accurate evidence cannot reverse injury.
- **Tavi:** defended identity → open testimony → sovereignty with bounded privacy → politically independent archivist who refuses to turn injury history into permanent exclusion.
- **Sev:** responsible risk officer → incompatible but sincere duties → temptation to use unjustified emergency action → actual material harm → acknowledgment and supervised service without automatic absolution.
- **Kala:** original aspiration to discover life → disciplined negative evidence → bounded experiments → stewardship of unresolved planetary ecology.
- **Sada:** protective silence → public testimony → painful encounter with the surviving archive → cross-generational life that does not cancel responsibility.
- **Miro, Aro, Daret, Holt, Ressa, Nyla:** each changes a material outcome rather than serving only as an exposition vehicle.

## Canonical technical guardrails

- Fixed VM81 **binary transport frame** = 81 × 64 bits = 5,184 bits = 648 bytes; separate fixed-width exact rational-scientific **serialized textual carrier** = 5,184 characters. Do not confuse bit size with character size.
- The 81 structural addresses are 72 peripheral and nine central. The 72nd peripheral address was administratively excluded, not physically absent.
- The 72×72 and 81×64 maps are alternative address factorizations for the normalized textual operand, not independent masses or spatial dimensions.
- Hash216 witness surface has distinct 72-character predecessor, change, and receipt lanes. Exact provenance does not certify the moral quality of an act.
- Ordered XY and YX readings are retained separately unless the specific equivalence has been proved for the state.
- Read-only planetary science observations and GPU/optimization candidates do not commit authority. No unapproved bypass of bounded VM81 admission.
- Mechanical safety remains subject to thermodynamics, finite fuel, human injury, maintenance and law.
- Alien ecology is not automatically personhood or consent. Human settlement cannot claim a signed response where none has been measured.
