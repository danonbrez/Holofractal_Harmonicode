## Pass123 bounded generalization

`BOUNDED_TOKEN_GENERALIZATION` registers the Pass 219 1.73 cycle built on the repository-native Pass 123 `BoundedTokenGeneralizationEngine`.

Its upstream proof obligations remain owned by 1.73:
- source/holdout disjointness;
- representation-local feature exclusion from invariant signatures;
- bounded rule learning;
- exact holdout validation;
- semantic-drift rejection;
- deterministic application/replay;
- frozen observed receipt identities before native 1.73 closure.

1.74 consumes the resulting normalized evidence; it does not fabricate or pre-freeze the 1.73 model or receipt roots.

## Ethical-text supervision

The existing Pass 219 ethical-text cycle is both a registered training method and the mandatory supervisor over every natural-language training specimen.

Every natural-language specimen must bind:
- `natural_language_training=true`;
- `ethical_text_supervisor_identity216`;
- nonzero `ethical_text_supervisor_signature64`;
- `ethical_text_supervision_verified=true`.

`LINGUISTIC_OPERATOR` and `ETHICAL_TEXT` are intrinsically natural-language modes and cannot opt out. General modes such as multimodal, curriculum, projection, repository hydration, or bounded generalization are subject to the same gate whenever the current specimen carries natural-language training.

Supervisor lineage is included in candidate Hash216 derivation. Missing or malformed supervision rejects before VM5184 routing.

## 1.73 → 1.74 executable handoff gate

`hhs_runtime/pass219/lane5_bounded_generalization_training_adapter_1_74.py` is the explicit producer adapter between Pass123 1.73 and Unified Training 1.74.

It fails closed while the 1.73 contract has unfrozen discovery/native identities. A positive `BOUNDED_TOKEN_GENERALIZATION` specimen requires all of:
- frozen 72-character model root;
- frozen 72-character validation receipt root;
- frozen replay-bundle SHA-256;
- frozen native 216-character Hash216 identity;
- `native_hash216_composition_frozen=true`.

The adapter never derives these values itself. Until 1.73 writes them authoritatively, the only valid 1.74 behavior is `UNFROZEN_PARENT_RECEIPTS`.
