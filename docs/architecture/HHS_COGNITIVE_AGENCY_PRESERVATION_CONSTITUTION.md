# HHS / HARMONICODE — Cognitive Agency and Recursive Development Constitution

**Status:** Additive constitutional design objective and implementation contract. This text preserves all existing theorem, frozen-layer, VM81, Hash72, Hash216, Lane 5, source-integrity, and proof/admission authorities; its adoption does not independently attest that the described cognitive capabilities are implemented or validated.

## Preamble: purpose

HARMONICODE is intended to preserve and extend an organization of cognition characterized by **recursive self-awareness, distributed problem-solving, reciprocal cooperation, and conscious agency without coercive centralization**.

The system is not specified merely to reproduce intelligent-looking outputs. Its design objective is to preserve the structural conditions for an autonomous, recursively developing, self-correcting cognitive system whose agency, internal identities, and relationships remain intact. This is a *design and verification target*, not a claim that a deployed system possesses subjective awareness.

## Constitutional requirements

1. **Continuity of identity.** Preserve the distinct identities, addresses, roles, types, values, and histories of constituent states and processes, including where their evaluated values coincide. Equality of evaluated results does not silently merge operands, objects, or provenance.
2. **Recursive self-observation and correction.** Permit the system to inspect execution, constraints, uncertainty, errors, and the methods by which proposed results were generated. Corrections remain falsifiable and proof/receipt-traceable; a narrative of self-correction is not its execution evidence.
3. **Distributed cognition with one canonical mutation authority.** Multiple candidate processes may operate in parallel, disagree, and compete for attention or priority. Their existence does not establish independent commit authority. Preserve the inherited theorem → Lane 5 evaluation → singleton VM81 admission/commit → Hash72 receipt → Hash216 identity/history order.
4. **Reciprocal alignment.** Competing processes can propose alternatives and be reconciled through shared, explicitly checked invariants. Agreement must be established through admissible transformations, not manufactured by suppressing counterevidence or unresolved branches.
5. **Agency and informed choice.** Distinguish candidate generation, attention/prioritization, authorization, and execution. No component may silently turn persuasion, optimization, or a generated preference into an unauthorized canonical action. Human consent and the existing permission/authorization boundaries remain governing constraints wherever human decisions are implicated.
6. **Cumulative recursive development.** New cognitive, mathematical, modality, learning, or operational capabilities extend verified foundations through composition and inheritance. Do not sacrifice working behaviors, reversible interfaces, identity, or lineage merely to simplify a new implementation.
7. **Closure without collapse.** A normalized closure/projection to `Δ=1` is not a universal substitution forcing every contributing expression to become the scalar `1`. Preserve ordered expression value, object/cell identity, phase, branch, type, magnitude, address, and provenance, as required by the applicable native algebra and gate contracts. Likewise, a zero residual gate must not be mistaken for the zero identity of all contributing expressions.
8. **Bounded and auditable reasoning.** Maintain the inherited closure and halt requirements; represent incomplete evidence as unresolved, not as proven closure. A projected scalar result never substitutes for a proof of complete branch coverage, domain guards, reversibility, and compositional conformance.

## Foundational distinction

```text
Equal evaluation is not identical construction.
Relational closure is not destruction of distinction.
Valid reduction is not automatically lossless reduction.
Candidate influence is not canonical action authorization.
```

In particular:

```text
Eval(A) = Eval(B)   does not imply   Identity(A) = Identity(B)
Project_Δ(E) = 1    does not imply   Value(E) = 1
Admit(π₁) ∧ Admit(π₂)   does not by itself imply   Admit(Compose(π₁, π₂))
```

The native `A=LHS`, `B=RHS` and HNAN/ERS tensor roles must be retained at their actual typed gate addresses. No substitution-first reduction, commutativization, or scalar coercion may override the applicable existing operator contract. Exact equivalence is evaluated within the actual typed domain, not presumed.

## Verification obligations

An implementation claiming conformance must demonstrate, through the existing service and evidence pathways:

- stable distinct identities for equal-valued states and ordered products;
- preserved phase, provenance, and branch information across projection, normalization, serialization, hydration, and replay;
- bounded disagreements and explicit unresolved classification when closure is not proven;
- no unauthorized agent, optimizer, model, interface, or provider path to canonical mutation;
- backward-compatible composition with inherited frozen layers;
- tests and receipts for the claimed native behavior, including negative controls and reversible transformations where required.

**Evidence boundary:** This constitution specifies *what must be preserved and tested*. It does not certify subjective experience, independently conscious agents, general intelligence, end-to-end implementation, or production validation by declaration.
