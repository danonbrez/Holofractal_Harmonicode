# Entropy-Neutral Information Transformation

## Abstract

HHS defines global entropy neutrality through exact recoverability of canonical information across transformation, compression, storage, branching, and replay.

## Criterion

For transformation `T`, neutrality requires a witnessed reconstruction map `R_T`:

\[
R_T(T(S),\operatorname{Receipt}(T))==S.
\]

Local representations may shrink, expand, or move across caches and parity shards. Global neutrality concerns the preservation of payload, semantic structure, ancestry, authority, and replay identity.

## Non-promotion

Compression efficiency, cache replication, or successful reconstruction does not increase proof authority.
