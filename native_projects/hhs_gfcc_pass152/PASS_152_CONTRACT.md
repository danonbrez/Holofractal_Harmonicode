## 4. Canonical symbolic foundation

The square-state sequence is:

```text
a² = 1
b² = 2
c² = b² + a² = 3
d² = c² + b² = 5
e² = d² + c² = 8
```

The dependency ancestry MUST remain available after evaluation.

Finite golden stages use the exact recurrence:

```text
S(n+1) = S(n) + S(n-1)
λ(n) = S(n+1) / S(n)
```

Finite ratios MUST retain independent integer numerator and denominator. They MUST NOT be silently replaced by an approximate floating-point Phi.

The symbolic limit is the positive root of:

```text
Phi² - Phi - 1 = 0
```

The orthogonal dyadic scale is the positive root satisfying:

```text
2 eta² = 1
```

The golden recurrence and dyadic projection remain independently addressable.

## 5. Shell-first closure

The numerator shell is:


and MUST close to `8`.

The denominator shell is:

```text

```

and MUST close to `4`.

Only after both shells close may the outer projection execute:


The scalar zero is the terminal projection, not a replacement for shell ancestry.

## 8. VM81 integration

VM81 is the recursive nonary lift `81 = 9 × 9`.

The canonical map is:

```text
i = 9r + c
(r,c) = divmod(i,9)
```

for `0 ≤ r,c < 9` and `0 ≤ i < 81`.

Forward and inverse maps MUST be exact, exhaustive, deterministic, and reversible.

Each cell MUST preserve index, row, column, nonary residue, phase lane, scale depth, parent, child mask, symbol, exact state, dependency, constraints, Hash72 projection reference, and Hash216 index reference.

No transition is authoritative unless it is admissible, dependency-resolved, constraint-closed, Hash72-projected, and receipt-recorded.

VM81 remains execution and transition authority.
