## Current SPI v8 papers

1. **HARMONICODE Octonion Reciprocal/Base-Pair Dimensional Lift Theorem**  
   `HARMONICODE_OCTONION_RECIPROCAL_BASEPAIR_DIMENSIONAL_LIFT_THEOREM.md`  
   Formalizes the distinct RML2 geometric-opposite, RML4 reciprocal-operand, and symbolic base-pair relations; D1–D4 relational lift; recursive D>4 same-algebra closure; typed `u^72` rotation round trip; and compatibility with `pi_L(a²)=1` without native collapse.

2. **HARMONICODE Computational Determinism and Bounded Execution Theorem**  
   `HARMONICODE_COMPUTATIONAL_DETERMINISM_AND_BOUNDED_EXECUTION_THEOREM.md`  
   Formalizes the bounded task envelope, `I_DET_COMPUTATIONAL_DETERMINISM`, exact `ADVANCE/HALT` domain, deterministic selection, receipt integrity/selection separation, HALT reason classes, and replay theorem.

3. **HARMONICODE Receipt-Bound Ethical Constraint Alignment Architecture**  
   `HARMONICODE_RECEIPT_BOUND_ETHICAL_CONSTRAINT_ALIGNMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`  
   Separates normative ethics, causal information, formal constraint construction, and deterministic enforcement. It describes how formally measurable ethical requirements can become receipt-bound computational constraints while explicitly not claiming a general solution to normative alignment or downstream prediction.
