## 9. Relation to the quadratic-reciprocity lattice proof

For distinct odd primes, classical quadratic reciprocity states:

```text
(p/q)(q/p)=(-1)^(((p-1)/2)((q-1)/2)).
```

The chalkboard construction counts lattice points in a rectangle and splits them by the diagonal `qx=py`. The parity of the two regions produces the reciprocity sign.

The HHS result here uses a related design principle — a discrete sign witness attached to ordered geometry — but the theorem is not derived from quadratic reciprocity. No comparative ranking between the two theorems is needed. Their domains and proof obligations are different.
