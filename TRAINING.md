## 1.73 bounded-generalization handoff

The merged 1.73 JSON contract intentionally does not publish a standalone `native_hash216_identity`. Instead it freezes the model/validation/replay identities and asserts `native_hash216_composition_frozen=true`; the native 1.73 cell wall derives and replay-checks its own `generalization_candidate_hash216`.

The 1.74 adapter therefore:

1. reads the three frozen 1.73 receipt identities and native-composition flag;
2. recomputes the Pass123 discovery and requires exact 12-rule, 12/12, zero-drift, 12-replay agreement;
3. asks the native 1.74 class to derive a candidate-only evidence Hash216 from the frozen tuple;
4. uses that derived evidence identity as method 19's source/oracle identity.

This 1.74 evidence identity is not represented as the 1.73 native candidate and does not become canonical Hash216 state.

## Ethical-text supervision

The existing Pass 219 ethical-text cycle is both a registered training method and the mandatory supervisor for every natural-language training specimen.

Every natural-language specimen must bind:
- `natural_language_training=true`;
- `ethical_text_supervisor_identity216`;
- a nonzero supervisor signature;
- `ethical_text_supervision_verified=true`.

`LINGUISTIC_OPERATOR` and `ETHICAL_TEXT` are intrinsically natural-language modes. All other methods are content-sensitive and inherit the same gate whenever their specimen contains natural-language training.

## Repository-wide producer registry

`training_specimens/HHS_LANE5_UNIFIED_TRAINING_REGISTRY_1_74.json` binds all 19 native method IDs to their existing producer implementations.

`hhs_runtime/pass219/lane5_unified_training_registry_1_74.py` rejects missing producers, method/order drift, duplicate modes/IDs, language-supervisor drift, or authority escalation. CI requires exact synchronization between this catalog and the built native C ABI.

## Cross-language adapters

The C façade and Python ctypes bridge are clients of the C++ class. Python, Wolfram, repository/PR tooling, corpus tooling, and external-library reconstruction remain producer adapters rather than independent training authorities.
