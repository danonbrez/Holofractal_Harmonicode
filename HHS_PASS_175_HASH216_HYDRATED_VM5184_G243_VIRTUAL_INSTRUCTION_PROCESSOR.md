# 6. Canonical dimensional invariants

\[
64\cdot81=72^2=5184.
\]

\[
G_{243}=\{0,1,2\}^{5},\qquad |G_{243}|=243.
\]

\[
5184\cdot243=1{,}259{,}712=2^6\cdot3^9.
\]

The 5,184 identities are permanent micro-operation addresses. The 243 controls are execution projections. Typed operands and immediate values SHALL NOT be collapsed into control identity.

# 7. Permanent micro-operation address

\[
\boxed{s=64c+o}
\]

where \(c\in[0,80]\), \(o\in[0,63]\), and \(s\in[0,5183]\).

The inverse SHALL be:

\[
c=\left\lfloor\frac{s}{64}\right\rfloor,\qquad o=s\bmod64.
\]

Every implementation SHALL prove all 5,184 round trips, uniqueness, coverage, and the canonical ring edge `5,183 → 0`.

# 8. Projected execution address

\[
\boxed{q=243s+g}
\]

where \(g\in[0,242]\) and \(q\in[0,1{,}259{,}711]\).

\[
s=\left\lfloor\frac{q}{243}\right\rfloor,\qquad g=q\bmod243.
\]

Every implementation SHALL prove all 1,259,712 projected round trips without skipped, duplicated, or aliased coordinates.

# 9. Five-trit control encoding

A control word SHALL contain exactly five ordered trits:

```text
g = (t0,t1,t2,t3,t4), each ti ∈ {0,1,2}
```

The encoding SHALL be canonical base-3 and reversible. Changing one trit changes control identity. Control operations SHALL retain source order and SHALL NOT be sorted or treated as a commutative multiset.

# 11. Ordered phase-decode fabric

The terminal implementation SHALL import the exact inherited table byte-for-byte, compute a table root, and prove that every operation retains its row, column, source symbols, ordered product, phase, and parenthesization.

# 12. Noncommutative ALU and reciprocal projection

\[
A=xy=(P^2,u^0),\qquad B=yx=(P^2,u^{36}).
\]

\[
A\neq_{\mathrm{instruction\ identity}}B.
\]

The guarded projection SHALL be:

\[
\operatorname{ProjectAB}(A,B)\rightarrow(P^4,u^0)
\]

only when all of the following validate:

- `A` is the ordered `xy` opcode;
- `B` is the ordered `yx` opcode;
- phases are exactly `0` and `36`;
- magnitudes are exactly `P²` and `P²`;
- source bytes and grouping are intact;
- predecessor, instruction, dependency, and provenance roots validate;
- the active privilege and exception classes permit projection;
- VM81 admits the candidate.

The output SHALL retain references to both input witness lanes. The compiler SHALL NOT erase `xy/yx` distinction merely because the scalar projection closes.

# 21. Conflict-safe parallelism

Two candidates MAY run concurrently only when:

\[
Write_i\cap Write_j=\varnothing,
\]
\[
Write_i\cap Read_j=\varnothing,
\]
\[
Write_j\cap Read_i=\varnothing.
\]

The scheduler SHALL version dependency roots and recheck them at admission. A candidate calculated from stale dependencies SHALL be rejected or deterministically recomputed.

