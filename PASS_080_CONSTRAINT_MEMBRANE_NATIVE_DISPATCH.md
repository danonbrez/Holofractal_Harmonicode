# Pass 080 — Constraint-Membrane Native Dispatch

## Governing transition

`RESOLVED_FOR_BOUNDED_INVOCATION` becomes exactly one of:

- `ADMIT_NATIVE_TRANSITION`
- `REJECT_NATIVE_TRANSITION_WITH_RECEIPT`
- `TYPED_UNAVAILABLE`
- `INDETERMINATE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION`

Admission terminates at `ADMITTED_FOR_LEASED_NATIVE_INVOCATION`. Pass 080 performs no native invocation and mutates no VM81 state.

## Implemented boundary

Pass 080 compiles the supplied equality, reciprocal, ordered-product, polynomial, radical, Lo Shu, VM81 orientation, plastic recurrence, QGU, and wave-denominator requirements into `HHS_NATIVE_CONSTRAINT_MEMBRANE_V1`.

Every one of the 29 Pass 079 native opcode contracts is bound to an `HHS_NATIVE_TRANSITION_ADMISSION_CONTRACT_V1`. Each contract commits its binding root, relation graph root, exact domain and residue requirements, authority and lease requirements, VM81 witness requirements, mutation bounds, and expected deterministic receipt schema.

## Exactness

All authoritative arithmetic is integer or `Fraction`-compatible rational arithmetic. Irrational recurrence identities are represented by exact polynomial-residue witnesses rather than floating approximations. Ordered `AB` and `BA` relations retain distinct lineage commitments even when their resolved values coincide.

## Admission receipt

Every non-exception decision emits `HHS_NATIVE_TRANSITION_ADMISSION_RECEIPT_V1`, binding the decision, admission contract, native binding, pre-state root, evaluated relation roots, exact observed residues, failure or success reason, and the explicit assertions:

- `native_execution_occurred = false`
- `native_state_mutated = false`
