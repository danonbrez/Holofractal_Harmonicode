## BOOK III — THE WORLD THAT ANSWERS BACK

### Chapter Thirty-Four — Consensus Expansion

Consensus expanded by succeeding locally.

That was the sentence Julian wrote at the top of the capability map.

Not by coup.

Not by secret decree.

Not because one mayor, company, party, agency, or engineer wanted total control.

Permits integrated because residents hated reentering identity.

Benefits integrated because duplicate documents delayed assistance.

Hiring integrated because credentials could be verified faster.

Education integrated because schools needed portable records.

Public alerts integrated because routing improved.

Emergency response integrated because the city needed one current address.

Content verification integrated because synthetic media was spreading.

Fraud integrated because relational graphs found patterns human clerks missed.

Each project had a meeting.

A legal review.

A privacy assessment.

A budget.

An owner.

A purpose.

An appeal procedure, usually.

A success metric.
