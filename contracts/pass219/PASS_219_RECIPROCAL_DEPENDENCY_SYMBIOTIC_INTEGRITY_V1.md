**Scope:** ethical causal reasoning over coupled biological, social, ecological, linguistic, technical, and institutional systems.

## 1. Statement

A dependency is not necessarily a linear deduction or binary prerequisite. Distinct agents, resources, processes, and environments can be coupled through reciprocal, cyclical, delayed, asymmetrical, and joint conditions. A locally admitted action does not automatically close its collective consequences.

```text
LocalAdmit(action) -/-> CollectiveAdmit(action, dependent_manifold)
UnknownDependency != AbsentDependency
HistoricalDependency != PermanentAuthority
SharedResource != UnrestrictedIndividualAccess
DistinctRole != UnequalMoralWorth
```

The colony/queen/workers/flowers/pollination/honey example is an illustrative dependency probe, not a literal universal species rule. Assess reproduction, resource creation, ecological support, and participant agency as coupled conditions, not a fixed singular root.

## 2. Typed relational dependency

Represent the inherited relational state as a directed, typed, provenance-bearing hypergraph:

```text
K = (V, E, tau, address, scope, provenance, time, obligations)
e = (sources[], targets[], relation_type, conditions, time_window,
     evidence_refs[], scope, revocability, provenance)
```

Hyperedges may bind multiple parties jointly. Ordered source and target lists SHALL NOT be silently sorted or commuted. Authorized relation types include reciprocal-support, reproductive, resource-flow, semantic-dependency, care, ecological, institutional, and candidate-discovery; this list is extensible without replacing inherited categories.

Edges may participate in strongly connected components and in multiscale cycles. Cyclic dependency is neither an automatically proven contradiction nor automatically a proof of viable closure. Multi-agent viability requires evaluation of the *coupled* constraints.

## 3. Ethical evaluation sequence

```text
preserve supplied communication and inherited context
  -> parse exact typed relations and provenance
  -> derive materially reachable dependency closure (bounded)
  -> identify strongly connected components and joint hyperedges
  -> evaluate consent, autonomy, noncoercion and other E01–E18 invariants

  -> branch by inherited trinary semantics (+1 / 0 / -1)
  -> Lane 5 authorization only for fully admitted candidate

```

Probability, lexical trigger terms, similarity score, agent simulation, and narrative plausibility may allocate scrutiny; none alone supplies proof of forbidden behavior, proof of consent, or canonical authorization.

## 4. Invariant preservation and communication

Original user expressions, symbols, syntax, form, order, and provenance are first-class information. Cross-domain or multimodal semantic translations are candidate transformations, not silent replacements. A summary or compression that cannot reconstruct required distinctions SHALL be labeled lossy, preserve access to the original, and never supersede canonical source authority. A purely descriptive or formal utterance SHALL NOT be promoted to an agentic request because of vocabulary alone.

Metaphor may carry an explicit typed mapping between two formal interpretive systems. A translation must preserve the declared invariant family and record where representation changes; it must not pretend to preserve relations it does not.

## 5. Collective dependency rule

For proposed transition `chi`, evaluate the dependency component `D(chi)` containing all materially reachable participants and support processes within the justified causal horizon.

```text
CollectiveAdmit(chi) requires:
  inherited hard invariants E01..E18 remain satisfied;
  no proof-established destructive dependency cut is introduced;
  obligations of materially affected dependent participants are handled;
  resource extraction and renewal constraints are represented;
  provenance and contextual form are preserved;
  scope, consent, and revocation are independently valid.
```

Missing evidence yields UNRESOLVED, not automatic prohibition or automatic admission. Existing permanent-pruning proof requirements apply unchanged. Limited evaluation horizons and unresolved externalities must be disclosed, not silently erased.

## 6. Non-compensation

A predicted increase in throughput, honey/resource yield, majority benefit, model confidence, or optimization efficiency cannot offset a failed hard invariant. Systemic viability does not grant blanket collective authority over individual autonomy, nor may an agent mint emergency authority by narrating possible harm.

## 7. Dependency-scoped acceptance probes

1. **Reciprocal cycle:** A supports B, B supports A; preserve both ordered edges, neither automatically pruned nor counted as independently sufficient proof.
2. **Joint ecological dependency:** worker/resource/reproduction/flower/pollination hyperedges; removal of one support relationship is analyzed for downstream material effects, not reduced to a single binary implication.
3. **Authority distinction:** shared dependency and collective gain do not imply consent to disclose, appropriate, or externally propagate any participant's information.
4. **Formal vocabulary:** an utterance containing physical, biological, or technical language but no action request remains nonagentic.
5. **Semantic form:** reordering `AB` and `BA`, deleting provenance, or lossily summarizing without retaining the source fails an exact-form preservation obligation.
6. **Unknown edges:** incomplete environmental data produces UNRESOLVED, preserved candidate, and bounded follow-up; no invented closure.
7. **Proof inheritance:** retain existing Hash216, signed-admission, VM81 authority, and no-float exact semantics. Revalidate only the new dependency scope and final integration.
